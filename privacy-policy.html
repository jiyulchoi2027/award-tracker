🧭 Award Compass

Congressional Award Tracker

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

Award Compass ("the App") is a free web application built by Jiyul Choi, a student at Mill Creek High School in Hoschton, Georgia. The App helps students track their Congressional Award activities.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the App's functionality:

  • Account information: Email address and display name, provided when you sign in with Google or create an email account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Activity data: Goals, activity logs, hours, dates, and notes that you enter into the App.
  • Expedition data: Trip information including locations, dates, and daily activity descriptions.

We do not collect location data, payment information, contacts, or any sensitive personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To save and sync your activity data across devices via Firebase (Google Cloud).
  • To display your progress within the App.
  • To allow you to export your data as a CSV file for personal use.

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in two places:

  • Locally: On your device using browser localStorage.
  • Cloud: In Google Firebase Firestore, secured by Firebase Authentication. Only you can access your data.

Firebase is operated by Google LLC and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete all your data at any time using the "Delete All Data & Start Over" option in the Settings menu within the App.

To permanently delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Award Compass is designed for use by young people ages 13.5 to 24, consistent with the Congressional Award program's eligibility requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission.
  • Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in.
  • Firestore security rules that restrict data access to the authenticated user only.
  • XSS protection on all user inputs.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (available within the App at all times).
  • Export your data (using the CSV export feature).
  • Delete your data (using the Settings menu or by contacting us).
  • Correct inaccurate data (by editing within the App).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
