# 🏅 Congressional Award Tracker

A free web app to help students track their Congressional Award activities — built by a student, for students.

---

## 👋 About This Project

My name is Kyle (Jiyul Choi), an 11th-grade student at Mill Creek High School in Hoschton, Georgia.

I arrived in the United States from South Korea in January 2024. As a recent immigrant navigating the Congressional Award program, I struggled to keep organized, consistent records of my activities across all four program areas.

This app was born from that frustration — and my desire to help other students, especially those new to the U.S., successfully complete the Congressional Award.

---

## 🎯 What This App Does

Helps students track progress across all 4 Congressional Award areas:

- 🤝 Voluntary Public Service
- 💪 Physical Fitness
- 📚 Personal Development
- 🧭 Expedition & Exploration

### Key Features
- Log activities by date and category
- Track hours toward Silver and Gold Medal goals
- Visualize progress with a simple dashboard
- Export records for Submittable submission
